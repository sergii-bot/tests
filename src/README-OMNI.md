# OMNI BRAIN — Workshop & Live Demonstrations

Local build. See `../README.md` for how to run it.

## New in v3
The start screen and menu have two direct entry points, **PARTS / WORKSHOP** and **KITCHEN / LIVE DEMONSTRATION**. Neither requires finishing the campaign.

### Build a robot
1. In the workshop, walk up to the assembly stand.
2. Choose a robot dog or a humanoid with the on-screen button, R or Space.
3. Check which module the stand needs next. Walk to that rack and press E.
4. Carry the part to the stand and press E to install it. You can carry one module at a time. G returns it to the rack.
5. After seven installs the robot activates and leaves the stand. You can build both types.

The dog uses four legs. The humanoid uses two legs and two arms. Both need a frame, a sensor head and a Brain. Co-op players can contribute to the same build.

### Watch the cooking demo
Choose the kitchen and walk up to the table. Two manipulators perform a 48-second sequence: they pick up an egg, crack it, cook it in the pan and move it to the plate with a spatula. Press E to restart the show for the whole room. You can walk freely around the table. The sizzle sound depends on distance and follows the global sound toggle.

The lab next door has animated corners for pour-over coffee and plant repotting. These are game interpretations of the demos, not video inserts.

## Controls
- WASD: move. Mouse or drag: look. E: interact.
- R: record, or choose the dog at the stand. Space: reinforce, or choose the humanoid at the stand.
- G: return part. Q: disable a joint in the adaptation lesson. V: follow-robot camera. Esc: menu.
- Arrow keys drive the gripper in manual lessons.

Touch screens get a joystick, a look area and buttons. On a gamepad, A is interact, X/Y are record/reinforce (body choice at the stand), and the right bumper returns a part.

## Campaign
Six lessons: carry demonstration M-01, precise placement M-02, route D-01, gaits D-02, adaptation D-03 and humanoid handover H-03. The menu has the journal, graphics quality and a reset of the shared Brain.

## Notes
This is a stylized browser game, not real ML training. It was inspired by https://www.skild.ai/blogs/s1. The fried-egg scene is an original staging.
